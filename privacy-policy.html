Privacy Policy

This page explains how NasPath may collect, use, and protect information when you visit this website.

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Introduction

NasPath is an independent resource hub created to help beginners learn about Nas.com, creator tools, community building, affiliate marketing, and online business workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains how NasPath may collect, use, and protect information when you visit this website.

Information we collect

At this stage, NasPath does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, payment details, or account login information through this website.

However, basic technical information may be collected automatically when you visit the site. This may include your browser type, device type, pages visited, referring website, approximate location, and other standard website usage information.

How we use information

Any information available through website hosting, analytics, affiliate tracking, or similar tools may be used to:

  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Improve the content and user experience
  • Fix technical issues
  • Protect the website
  • Measure the performance of resources or affiliate links

Affiliate links and third-party websites

NasPath may include affiliate links or links to third-party websites, tools, platforms, or services. If you click a third-party link, you will leave NasPath and visit a website that is not controlled by NasPath.

Third-party websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies. NasPath is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or actions of third-party websites.

GitHub Pages hosting

NasPath is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process certain technical information when visitors access websites hosted through its services. You can review GitHub’s privacy practices through GitHub’s own privacy documentation.

Cookies and tracking

NasPath may use cookies or similar technologies in the future for analytics, affiliate tracking, or website improvement. A cookie is a small file stored by your browser that helps websites remember information or measure activity.

At this time, NasPath does not intentionally use advanced tracking systems directly on the website unless added later.

Email and contact forms

If NasPath adds an email signup form, contact form, newsletter, or lead magnet in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and how it is used.

Children’s privacy

NasPath is intended for general educational and business-related use. It is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and NasPath does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

NasPath aims to use reasonable steps to protect any information connected with this website. However, no website, hosting service, or online transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You can choose not to click affiliate links, not to submit personal information through future forms, and adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies.

Updates to this policy

NasPath may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the website grows, adds new tools, or changes how information is handled. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact NasPath through the contact method provided on this website once available.
